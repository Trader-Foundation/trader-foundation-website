# Typed material

For teaching that exists in Vlad's head rather than on a recording. Psychology
is the first case: the live sessions assume it and rarely stop to teach it.

**Typed beats transcribed here.** No transcription errors to chase, and
`corpus/schema.md` already handles written sources: they carry a section
heading instead of a timestamp, and they are authoritative for numbers because
nobody garbled them.

## How to write one

Plain markdown. One file per topic. `##` headings become the citation, so make
them the thing a student would ask about.

```
# Revenge trading

## What it is
Naming the thing, in the words a coach would use.

## How it shows up
The behaviour, concretely. Sizing up after a loss, entering without a setup.

## What to do instead
The instruction. Something the student can act on tonight.
```

Cited as: **Revenge trading, "How it shows up"**.

## Rules

- **No dollar figures and no rates of return.** Same non-negotiables as
  everywhere else. The build filter runs on this too.
- **Write it as spoken.** The bot should sound like a coach, and typed prose
  drifts formal. Short sentences win.
- **Nothing here needs a timestamp.** That is the point of the written path.
